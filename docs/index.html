Move FI-to-FI credit transfers with confidence.

pacs008 is an open-source toolkit that generates, validates and audits ISO 20022 pacs.008 payment messages — with scheme-aware rules for CBPR+, HVPS+, Fedwire, CHAPS, T2 RTGS and SCT Inst. Cut repair rates, raise straight-through processing, and stay ahead of the November 2026 structured-address deadline.

The coexistence window is closed. Data quality is the job now.

The cross-border coexistence period for payment instructions ended on 22 November 2025. From here, the risk is no longer whether you send ISO 20022 — it is whether your party data, addresses and amounts survive validation without manual repair. In November 2026, unstructured postal addresses begin to be rejected in key payment messages. pacs008 is built for exactly this: clean structured data, in and out, verifiable before you ever hit the network.

Fewer repairs

JSON Schema, IBAN checksum and XSD validation catch malformed party data before submission, so payments clear straight through instead of queuing for manual repair.

Secure by construction

XXE-safe XML parsing via defusedxml, strict path-traversal allow-listing, and PII masking in structured logs for GDPR and PCI DSS alignment.

2026-ready party data

Structured and hybrid PostalAddress27 handling, plus stronger debtor, creditor and agent validation for the November 2026 address requirements.

One suite, three composable packages

Adopt only what you need. Every package is Apache-2.0, typed (py.typed), and published on PyPI.

Scheme-aware, not just schema-aware

A message that validates against the XSD can still be wrong for the scheme carrying it. pacs008 encodes the rules each market actually enforces — charge bearers, UETR requirements, batch cardinality, address policy and version pinning — so a payment is right for its rail, not merely well-formed.
