Privacy policy

pacs008.com is a privacy-first static website. We collect as little as technically possible, and nothing at all beyond what a standard web host records or what you choose to send us.

Last updated: 26 July 2026.

The short version

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, embeds no third-party trackers, and performs no fingerprinting. It is served as plain static files. The only information that ever leaves your browser is what you voluntarily submit through the contact form, or an email you decide to send us.

No cookies, no analytics, no trackers

We do not use cookies or local storage for tracking. A theme preference (light or dark) may be stored locally in your browser purely to remember your choice; it never leaves your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. We do not load analytics scripts, advertising pixels, social widgets or any other third-party code. There is nothing on this site attempting to identify or profile you.

Static hosting and server logs

The site is hosted as static files (for example, on GitHub Pages or an equivalent host). Like almost every web server, the host may keep standard access logs that can include your IP address, the requested URL, a timestamp and your browser’s user-agent string. These logs are generated and controlled by the hosting provider for security and operational purposes, not by us, and we do not combine them with any other data. Please refer to the relevant host’s own privacy documentation for how long such logs are retained.

The contact form

If the contact form is configured, submitting it sends the details you type — your name, email address, optional organisation and message — to Formspree, a form-processing service, which forwards them to us by email. In that case your submission is processed by Formspree under its own privacy policy and terms. We use the information you send solely to reply to you. If you would rather not use the form, you can email us directly instead.

Emails you send us

If you email contact@pacs008.com, we receive and keep that message so we can respond and, where relevant, follow up. We do not add your address to any mailing list.

Your rights

Because we hold so little, there is usually very little to act on — but you can still ask us. You have the right to request access to any personal data we hold about you and to request its erasure. To exercise these rights, email contact@pacs008.com and we will respond as promptly as we reasonably can.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after an update means the revised policy applies.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email contact@pacs008.com.
